Event Description

What can insects reveal about crime? Explore the facinating world of forensic entomology, where science and investigation meet, with one of our university's leading entomological experts.

What to Expect

Attendees can expect an engaging introduction to forensic entomology and its role in criminal investigations. The symposium will explore how insects help investigators estimate time since death, interpret environmental clues, and collect evidence, with insights from experts who connect biology, law enforcement, and the pursuit of justice.

Event Schedule

Lectures will be open to all students from 9am to 3pm with a break for lunch around 2pm.

  1. Melinda Thomas, PhD | Introduction
  2. Finnian Copeland, PhD | Bioaccumulation in Maggots
  3. Gilbert Hunt, PhD | Advances in Entomotoxicology
